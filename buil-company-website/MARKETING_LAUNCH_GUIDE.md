# Mila Interior Solutions — Quick Marketing Launch Guide

## Public links

Use the working public website link in advertisements: https://www.mila-interior-solutions.com/ . The Manus fallback link is https://builweb-m4zaxxc3.manus.space/ . The `www` custom domain is the verified HTTPS address; the bare root domain still needs Manus root-domain SSL activation before it should be promoted.

## What customers can do now

Customers can browse All Stickers and use the custom sticker calculator. They can enter multiple measurements, choose a separate sticker quality for each side, select delivery, and place an order. Cash on Delivery is the primary payment method. bKash, Nagad, Rocket, and bank transfer are also listed. Card, Stripe, and Shopify card payment remain inactive and should not be advertised as available.

Use a short bilingual message such as: “আপনার Fridge, Freezer, Wardrobe বা Door-এ নতুন look দিন। Choose your design, enter your measurements, and order with Cash on Delivery. Visit: https://www.mila-interior-solutions.com/”

For service enquiries, direct customers to WhatsApp: +880 1404 225856. Do not promise fixed installation dates or prices unless an admin has confirmed the measurement, delivery area, material quality, and service availability.

## Owner pre-launch check

Before running paid ads, open the website from a mobile phone, test the All Stickers page, open the sticker calculator, verify that COD is selected, and confirm that the WhatsApp button opens the correct number. Keep the owner email and Manus OAuth account secure. Do not publish customer photos, payment screenshots, order exports, passwords, or OTPs in advertisements.

## Current limitations

The Shop catalog currently shows that products are still being prepared, so initial marketing should focus on the sticker calculator, custom sticker orders, and WhatsApp service enquiries. Real product prices, stock, Shopify checkout, and card payment should be promoted only after the owner configures and tests them.

## Fast operating routine

Review new orders daily in Admin → Orders, confirm measurements and delivery details before fulfilment, keep Finance entries based on real transactions, and respond to WhatsApp enquiries from the owner-controlled business account. Use the website’s Track an order flow for customer status requests.
